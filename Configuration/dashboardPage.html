Keys and tips
Scroll over the picture, the frames or the timeline to step one frame (Shift: a second, Ctrl: a keyframe). Drag the frames below the picture (or their scroll bar) to look along them without moving; the frame you're on has a yellow frame, and a yellow button brings you back to it. Keys: ← → frame, Shift+← → second, ↑ ↓ keyframe, , . black, [ ] break start and end, Space play / stop, J K L reverse / stop / play (again: faster: 1×, 2×, 4×, 8×, 16×, 32×). Sound plays forward up to 4×; 16× and 32× show a picture at every keyframe.
To mark a new break, press Break starts here where it starts (it lights up), go to where it ends and press Break ends here; either order works, and pressing the lit button again cancels. Inside a break, the buttons move that break's start or end. A new break that overlaps others joins them.
Point at the timeline to see the picture there; click, or drag along it to scrub. Drag a break's edges to move its start or end. Blue along the top: other segments (intros, a program-start preview), not edited here. On a cut recording, green marks are where the cut joined the parts it kept. Picture (top right) sets the picture's size, or drag its bottom-right corner; Fit the window keeps the controls and the timeline in view.
